# Formats

Formats are repeatable shapes for posts. Rotating them keeps the feed from feeling samey, and each one suits a different funnel stage. Post structures for each are in `post-structure.md`.

| Format | What it is | Best funnel stage | Use when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Story and lesson | A real moment, what happened, what it taught you | Top | You have a specific story with a clear turn |
| Contrarian take | Common advice, why it's wrong for your buyer, what works | Top or middle | The user holds a real, defensible position |
| Framework | A 3 to 5 step method, tied to the moment that created it | Middle | The user has a repeatable way of doing something |
| List | "7 things I'd tell a first-time founder in our space" | Top | Lots of small lessons on one theme |
| Before and after | Where things were, the change, where they are now | Top or middle | There's a clear transformation with numbers |
| Behind the scenes | A real decision, mistake or number from building the company | Middle | The user is open about how the company runs |
| Customer result | A customer's trigger, what changed, the result | Bottom | The user has a result they're allowed to share |
| Objection answered | A doubt buyers raise, answered honestly with proof | Bottom | The interview surfaced a common reason deals stall |
| Teardown | A common mistake in the buyer's world, broken down step by step | Middle | The user sees the same mistake again and again |

## Visuals

People see the image before they read a word, so the visual does half the hook's job. Match it to the post:

| Post type | Visual | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal story | A real photo of the founder, ideally in the moment | Faces stop the scroll, and a real photo makes the story believable |
| A result or news | A screenshot (a dashboard, an email, a message, with private details blurred) | It's proof the reader can see |
| Framework or list | A simple graphic or a short carousel (document post) | People save things they want to come back to |
| Contrarian take | Often no image, or a plain text graphic of the claim | The words do the work |

Skip stock photos. Readers spot them, and they say "I didn't have a real one". Portrait images (4:5) take up more of the feed than landscape ones.

## Rotation rules

- No two posts in a row in the same format.
- At least one story post a week. Stories build trust faster than anything else.
- Bottom of funnel formats (customer result, objection answered) no more than once a week, and after a trust post on the same topic where possible.
